# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in the OpenRig community a welcoming and respectful experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, or technical expertise.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Being respectful, constructive, and considerate
- Giving and receiving technical feedback professionally
- Focusing criticism on ideas, code, and decisions rather than individuals
- Acknowledging mistakes and learning from differing perspectives
- Respecting privacy and never sharing confidential or personal information

## Unacceptable behavior

Examples include harassment, discrimination, intimidation, personal attacks, doxxing, publishing private information without consent, and deliberately disruptive conduct.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in project repositories, issue trackers, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces where someone represents the OpenRig project.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may restrict participation for behavior they consider inappropriate, threatening, or harmful.

To report a concern, contact the repository maintainer privately through the contact method listed on their GitHub profile. Do not post personal or sensitive information publicly.
